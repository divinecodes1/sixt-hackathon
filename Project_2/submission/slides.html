THE METHOD

Predict with the information
available at the time.

1 · Establish the timeline

Retimed readings + recorded availability for mileage, rentals and category.

2 · Compare simple and rental-aware models

Choose on validation; calibrate ranges on a separate later period.

3 · Deliver an estimate and a review queue

Per-vehicle output, age of last reading, uncertainty and explainable flags.

Stable vehicle holdout + later periods. No LLM or paid inference. Future-field mutation tests protect the boundary.
