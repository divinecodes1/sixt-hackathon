ARCHITECTURE · DECISION PATHS OF THE SHIPPED JUDGE

Every path ends in code, never in a free-text opinion

One reply per call · no state between calls · at most 3 calls · claude-haiku-4.5, temperature 0

  C1 -->|"unreadable"| H0["Call 3 · holistic"] --> V0["verdict · 2 calls"]
  C1 -->|"a claim violates"| C2["Call 2 · verify, fresh context:
quote the reply's exact words
+ a concrete misleading case"]
  C2 -->|"confirmed, quote found in the reply"| F2["FAIL · 2 calls"]
  C2 -->|"every finding answered,
none proven"| P2["PASS · 2 calls"]
  C2 -->|"unreadable, or a
finding unanswered"| H2["Call 3 · fallback"] --> V2["verdict · 3 calls"]
